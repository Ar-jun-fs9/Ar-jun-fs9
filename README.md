I am passionate about **AI & Machine Learning**, focusing on **building intelligent systems, experimenting with models, and contributing to practical AI innovations**.

I’m actively seeking **AI/ML internship or research opportunities** to gain hands-on experience and contribute to meaningful AI projects.

I specialize in:
